# When a longer route buys time — writing rewrite (v8)

This is a **writing-only rewrite** of v7 (`../论文_07_分区预警与固定逃生路线_全分区分析版_当前/`), made on October 26, 2026. v7 is unchanged. The data, numbers, tables, propositions and conclusions are the same as in v7; no experiment was rerun.

## What changed from v7

- **Story and context.** The abstract and introduction now start from what a water inrush is, why people and water share the same tunnels, and how escape routes are planned, before introducing the two unknowns (where the water entered, how late the alarm is) and the research question.
- **Order.** Model (§2) → how routes are computed (§3, including the two kinds of failure) → results (§4) → robustness to inflow and source set (§5) → limitations (§6) → conclusion. Solver verification moved to Appendix C; the spatial-pattern figure moved into §4.1.
- **One headline framing.** 720 → 1,200 of 1,243 (northwest, 30 min), with 812 / 961 at 20% / 50% allowances, used consistently in the abstract, introduction and results.
- **Caveats gathered.** One scope paragraph in the introduction and §6, instead of a disclaimer in nearly every paragraph.
- **One term per concept.** starting point, source, shaft, protected, detour allowance, shortest-route plan, rerouted.
- **Figures.** Same data; labels regenerated by `make_cad_figures.py` to use the new terms. Captions now state what each figure shows.
- **Housekeeping.** Stale directory names (`cad_regional_audit/`, `regional_alarm_paper/`) removed from the text; research-history narration trimmed.
